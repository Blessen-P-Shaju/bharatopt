# BharatOPT Multi-Period Refinery Scheduling & Supply Chain Logistics

## 1. Logistics Planning Hierarchy
Bridging strategic monthly linear programming plans with operational daily batch scheduling.

### Multi-Period Discrete Time Representation
Partitioning planning horizons into daily or shift time buckets.

### Tank Farm Dynamic Mass Balance Equations
Stock equation balancing inventory carry-over with receipts and withdrawals.

### Working Ullage and Minimum Heel Constraints
Physical tank limits preventing pump cavitation and overflow.

### Dedicated vs Flexible Service Tank Allocation
Binary decision variables assigning crude or product grades to tanks.

### Tank Residence Time and Settling Requirements
Enforcing dewatering settling time before crude tank transfer.

### Crude Tanker Arrival Scheduling
Jetty berth allocation for VLCC and Suezmax vessels.

### Demurrage Cost Function Formulation
Piecewise linear penalty on vessel discharge delays exceeding laytime.

# BharatOPT Clean Fuels Regulatory Standards & Blending Formulations

## 1. Statutory Regulatory Frameworks
Covers Indian Bharat Stage VI (BS-VI), European Euro-6, and US EPA Tier 3 standards.

### BS-VI Automotive Gasoline Specifications
RON >= 91/95, MON >= 81/85, Sulfur <= 10 ppm.

### BS-VI High Speed Diesel Specifications
Cetane Number >= 51, Cetane Index >= 46, Density @ 15°C: 820-845 kg/m3, Sulfur <= 10 ppm.

### Nonlinear Octane Blending - Nelson Blending Numbers
Accounting for antagonistic and synergistic interactions.

### Reid Vapor Pressure (RVP) Blending Methods
Chevron RVP Index formulation: RVP^1.25 linear on volume fraction.

### Distillation Cut Points Blending (E70, E100, E150)
Predicting gasoline driveability index.

### Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF / Jet A-1) Specifications
Smoke point >= 25 mm, freeze point <= -47°C, flash point >= 38°C.

### Marine Bunker Fuel IMO 2020 Compliance
VLSFO 0.50% sulfur cap and stability blending.

### Cold Filter Plugging Point (CFPP) Blending in Diesel
Winter grade diesel blending with kerosene.

# BharatOPT Crude Oil Assay Catalog & True Boiling Point Analysis

## 1. Introduction
Accurate crude oil assay characterization is fundamental to refinery linear programming.

### Crude Classification Framework
Classifying crudes by API gravity and Sulfur content.

### True Boiling Point (TBP) Distillation Definition
ASTM D2892 15-theoretical-plate batch distillation curves.

### Arab Light Operational Characteristics
Benchmark Saudi crude yielding balanced middle distillates.

### Arab Heavy Operational Characteristics
High-yield residue crude requiring delayed coking and RFCC.

### Brent Blend Operational Characteristics
Light sweet North Sea benchmark with low sulfur and high gas oil.

### Forties Operational Characteristics
Light crude with excellent reforming naphtha qualities.

### Maya Heavy Sour Operational Characteristics
High-TAN and high-metals crude requiring specialized metallurgy.

### WTI Midland Operational Characteristics
Ultra-low sulfur light crude producing premium reformate blendstocks.

### Mumbai High Sweet Paraffinic Characteristics
Indian offshore crude with high pour point requiring heated storage.

### Mangala High-Wax Rajasthan Characteristics
Waxy inland Indian crude with exceptionally high vacuum gasoil yields.

### Ravva KG Basin Crude Characteristics
Premium Indian sweet crude ideal for direct kerosene and ATF production.

### Murban UAE Extra-Light Characteristics
High-API crude with high yields of light naphtha and aviation kerosene.

### Dubai Fateh Middle East Benchmark Characteristics
Medium sour crude used as standard reference for Middle East pricing.

### Basrah Medium Iraqi Crude Characteristics
Medium sour crude with high middle distillate recovery.

### Bonny Light Nigerian Crude Characteristics
Low-sulfur paraffinic crude yielding excellent low-emissions diesel.

### Urals Russian Blend Characteristics
Medium sour crude widely processed across Asian refining complexes.

### Castilla Colombian Heavy Characteristics
Extra-heavy asphaltic crude yielding high bitumen and vacuum residue.

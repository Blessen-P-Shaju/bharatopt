# BharatOPT Crude Oil Assay Catalog & True Boiling Point Analysis

## 1. Introduction
Accurate crude oil assay characterization is fundamental to refinery linear programming.

### Crude Classification Framework
Classifying crudes by API gravity and Sulfur content.

### True Boiling Point (TBP) Distillation Definition
ASTM D2892 15-theoretical-plate batch distillation curves.

### Arab Light Operational Characteristics
Benchmark Saudi crude yielding balanced middle distillates.

### Arab Heavy Operational Characteristics
High-yield residue crude requiring delayed coking and RFCC.

### Brent Blend Operational Characteristics
Light sweet North Sea benchmark with low sulfur and high gas oil.

### Forties Operational Characteristics
Light crude with excellent reforming naphtha qualities.

### Maya Heavy Sour Operational Characteristics
High-TAN and high-metals crude requiring specialized metallurgy.

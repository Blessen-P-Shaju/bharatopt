# BharatOPT Decarbonization, Energy Management & Carbon Accounting

## 1. Overview
Energy consumption constitutes up to 60-70% of refinery operating expenditure (OPEX).

### Refinery Energy Balance Fundamentals
First and second law thermodynamic balances across fired heaters and boilers.

### Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) Metrics
Gigajoules per metric ton of crude processed (GJ/t).

### Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) PAT Scheme
Perform, Achieve and Trade mandatory energy reduction targets.

### Energy Saving Certificates (ESCerts) Trading
Economic valuation of ESCerts earned by beating PAT targets.

### Solomon Energy Intensity Index (EII) Methodology
Standardized complexity-weighted benchmarking against peers.

### Fired Heater & Furnace Thermal Efficiency
Stack gas temperature, excess oxygen control, and air preheater recovery.

### Soot Blowing Optimization in Convection Sections
Scheduling automated soot blowers to prevent convective tube fouling.

### Steam Header Balance & Turbine Letdown Optimization
HP, MP, and LP steam header balances.

### Backpressure vs Condensing Turbine Selection
Optimizing electric power generation vs process steam demand.

### Steam Trap Auditing & Loss Elimination
Quantifying steam trap failure rates and acoustic monitoring.

### Heat Exchanger Network (HEN) Pinch Analysis
Composite curves and maximum heat recovery.

### Preheat Train Crude Fouling Mitigation
Optimizing chemical antifoulant dosing and cleaning campaigns.

### Fuel Gas System Balancing & Flare Minimization
Balancing off-gas with natural gas make-up.

### Flare Gas Recovery System (FGRS) Compressor Operations
Liquid ring compressors recovering flare gas into fuel gas header.

### LPG Recovery from Off-Gas Streams
Cryogenic and absorption recovery of propane and butane fractions.

### Hydrogen Network Pinch Analysis
Hydrogen source-sink composite curves identifying minimum fresh generation.

### Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA) Hydrogen Recovery
Multi-bed PSA units recovering 99.9% pure hydrogen.

### Membrane Separation for Hydrogen Recovery
Polymeric hollow-fiber membranes for bulk hydrogen separation.

### Electrolytic Green Hydrogen Integration
Modeling solar-powered PEM electrolyzers for refinery decarbonization.

### Captive Power Plant (CPP) Combined Cycle Optimization
Gas turbine heat recovery steam generators heat rate minimization.

### Electric Motor Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs)
Energy savings from pump and fan speed modulation.

### Cooling Water Tower Pumping Optimization
Cell staging and wet-bulb temperature tracking.

### Desalter Heat Optimization
Pre-heating crude desalter feed to 125-135°C to maximize separation.

### Atmospheric Column Pumparound Heat Recovery
Maximizing heat rejection to cold crude rather than air coolers.

### Vacuum System Motive Steam Reduction
Replacing multi-stage steam ejectors with hybrid liquid-ring systems.

### FCCU Power Recovery Train (PRT)
Flue gas expansion turbines generating mechanical power from regenerator gas.

### Carbon Accounting Framework: Scope 1 Emissions
Direct emissions from fuel combustion, coke burn-off, and SMR reforming.

### Carbon Accounting Framework: Scope 2 Emissions
Indirect emissions from imported electricity and commercial steam.

### Carbon Accounting Framework: Scope 3 Emissions
Value chain emissions including shipping and downstream product combustion.

### IPCC Tier 3 Emission Factor Methodologies
Fuel-specific carbon content and oxidation factors.

### Continuous Emission Monitoring Systems (CEMS)
Online flue gas analyzers measuring SO2, NOx, CO, and CO2.

### Carbon Capture, Utilization & Storage (CCUS) Readiness
Amine-based post-combustion CO2 capture from stacks.

### CO2 Utilization in Enhanced Oil Recovery (EOR)
Pipelines delivering captured CO2 to mature oilfields.

### Internal Carbon Pricing in Capital Expenditure
Shadow carbon price ($50-$80/t CO2) integrated into project evaluations.

### Biofuel Co-Processing in Hydrotreaters
Co-processing 5-10% used cooking oil into renewable diesel.

### Compressed Biogas (CBG) Integration
Injecting CBG from agricultural waste into refinery fuel gas networks.

### Solar Photovoltaic Microgrid in Refinery Buffer Zones
Deploying ground-mounted solar PV in greenbelts.

### Waste Heat to Power Organic Rankine Cycle (ORC)
Generating electricity from low-grade waste heat.

### Thermal Insulation Auditing & Heat Loss Prevention
Infrared thermography surveys to replace damaged insulation.

### Decarbonization Abatement Cost Curves
Ranking energy conservation projects by $/t CO2 abated.

### Refinery Water Pinch & Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)
Water re-use networks, reverse osmosis permeate recycling.

### Cooling Tower Blowdown Re-use in Demineralization
Reducing freshwater withdrawal from municipal sources.

### Sludge Dewatering & Bio-Remediation Economics
Thermal desorption and bio-slurry treatment of tank bottoms.
